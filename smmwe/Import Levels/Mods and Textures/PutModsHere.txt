Put mods here. Subfolders are fine.

Textures (.zip): in the game go to Settings and press the button to import
textures. The zips in this folder show up in the list.

Music and sounds (.ogg, or a .zip full of .ogg files): they replace the game
sounds with the same name. They are added when you start the port (about one
minute, only when something here changed). Delete them to get the original
sounds back.

Only sounds and textures can be modded. PC mods that change the game itself
(data.win, .exe, .dll) do not work on this port.
